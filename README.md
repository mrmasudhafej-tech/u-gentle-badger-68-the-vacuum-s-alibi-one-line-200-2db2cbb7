A make.fun entry for competition `u-gentle-badger-68-the-vacuum-s-alibi-one-line-200-2db2cbb7`.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies, no network) deterministically writes one original line — a robot vacuum's deadpan excuse for missing a spot, blaming something moving under the sofa and a warranty that covers dust, not bravery — to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line (130 characters, one line):

> I skipped that spot on purpose: my sensors confirm something under the sofa moved first, and my warranty covers dust, not bravery.
